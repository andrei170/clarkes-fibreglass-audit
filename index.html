You said the online side brings you very little. Here is the first reason why: on a phone, the number in your header is not a phone link.

Your reputation is already there. 10 out of 10 on Checkatrade, Sika approved, and £20,000 jobs. What is missing is everything between a stranger finding you and picking up the phone: a working call button, proof on the page, and any way to measure what happens.

Site read from its own served pages, 17–18 September 2026
Finding 1 — The Header Number Is An Email Link
The number at the top of your homepage, in the page code: <a href="mailto:%20tclarkesfibreglassroofing@gmail.com"> <p>07523 447 536</p> </a> What it should be: <a href="tel:07523447536">07523 447 536</a> Your footer number: correct — it dials Contact page: "24 Hour Emergency No. 07523447536" is plain text, not a link
Somebody standing under a leaking roof taps the number and gets an email screen

The most visible number on the site does not ring you. On a phone it opens a blank email instead, and that email address has a stray space in it. The people most likely to tap a number at the top of a roofing website are the urgent ones — and the emergency number on your contact page is not tappable either.

This is a ten-minute fix and it is the single most valuable thing on this page.

Finding 2 — Your Reviews Are Excellent And Invisible
Checkatrade: 10 out of 10 · 17 reviews · member since February 2024 Sika Approved badge shown on your profile On your own website: Reviews or testimonials: none, on any of the 5 pages Checkatrade badge or score: not shown
A £20,000 job is a trust purchase

Somebody spending twenty thousand pounds on a flat roof is checking whether you are safe to let onto their building. You have a perfect Checkatrade score and it appears nowhere on your own site. Your Sika approval is on the homepage, which is good — the reviews should be sitting right next to it.

Finding 3 — Five Pages, And Not One Names A Town
Pages on clarkesfibreglassroofing.com: 5 (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact) Pages naming a town you work in: 0 Sussex Flat Roofing: 40 town pages Seaview Roofing: ~18 town pages JAH Roofing: 5 Singers Roofing: 3 Your contact page says: "Working Throughout Sussex"
This is where the qualified local work comes from

Somebody in Worthing, Shoreham or Chichester searching for a flat roofer is shown pages about those towns. You have none, and the firms around you have between three and forty. You said you want more local jobs — this is the cheapest way to get in front of them, and your gallery already has the photos to fill the pages.

Finding 4 — Nothing Is Being Measured
Google Analytics ID sent to the browser: none Google Tag Manager: none Google Ads tag: none Meta pixel: none Google's Site Kit plugin: installed — but no measurement ID is being served
The tool is on the shelf, not plugged in

The plugin that would measure your website is installed but not sending anything. So there is no way to know how many people visit, which pages they read, or how many tried to contact you — which makes "the online side does not bring much in" impossible to prove or fix.

Finding 5 — The Quote Form Asks Nothing About The Roof
Your homepage quote form: Name · Email · Phone What a £20,000 flat roof enquiry needs: What kind of roof? (GRP / liquid / single ply / repair) ......... so you know the job Roughly how big? (garage / extension / whole commercial roof) ... so you know the value Where is it? (town or postcode) ................................. so you can say yes or no New roof or a problem with an existing one? ...................... urgent or planned A photo ......................................................... so you can price before driving
Three fields brings you everybody. Five brings you buyers.

You told our team you want more qualified jobs, not more jobs. The form is where that filtering happens — and it is also what stops you driving across Sussex to price a job that was never a £20,000 job.

Small Fixes While You Are Here
None of these lose a job alone. Together they read as unfinished.
  • Your footer says "© 2025 Clarkes Fibre Glass Roofing" — last year, and the brand spelled differently from your own domain.
  • A typo on the homepage and services page: "Regardless of your requirements are team will always be on hand."
  • Your Facebook icon links to a single photo post, not to your Facebook page.
  • One gallery image is 206 pixels wide and stretched to fill the space, and another is stored sideways and displays rotated.
  • The homepage loads around 4.5 MB, mostly large screenshots, which slows it on a phone.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Make every number dial. Header, contact page, emergency number. Ten minutes, and it is losing calls today.
  • Put the 10/10 and the reviews on the site, next to the Sika approval.
  • Switch measurement on, so calls and form fills are counted from day one.
  • Rebuild the quote form to ask roof type, size, town and a photo.
  • Build a page for each town you want work in, using jobs you have already done there.
  • Then advertise, judged on booked surveys and jobs won, not clicks.

The reputation is built. The website is the part that is not finished.

Most of this is days of work, not a new site.
